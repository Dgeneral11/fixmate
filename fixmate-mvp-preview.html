For artisans

A clear review process

Evidence depends on the trade and the reason for the check. It is reviewed privately; it is not collected by this HTML preview.

1
FixMate contacts you

WhatsApp may be used to request missing information, arrange a physical meeting, check trade experience, or explain the process.

2
Provide relevant evidence

Identity details, proof of trade skills, association credentials where applicable, and other relevant evidence may be requested.

3
Administrator review

An administrator privately reviews the evidence and records the decision in FixMate.

4
Approval enables verified features

Only after approval is the artisan marked verified and eligible for applicable verified-provider features.

FixMate does not issue government licences. Any official trade credential must be confirmed independently and displayed separately from the FixMate verification badge.
